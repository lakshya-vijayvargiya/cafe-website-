Our Heritage, Mission & Evolution

Since 2024, The Bidholi Corner (TBC) has grown from a humble campus dream into a beloved culinary landmark near UPES Dehradun. Spanning dual energetic hubs in Bidholi and Kandoli, our foundation rests on creating a genuine "third place"—where academic pursuits blend effortlessly with slow coffee rituals and warm social connection.

Our philosophy treats hospitality as an art form: combining artisanal bean profiling with multi-cuisine comfort favorites (from wood-fired sourdough pizzas to wok-tossed Asian street plates). Driven by Team Code Minds, we bridge physical comfort with digital-forward convenience, serving thousands of students, faculty, and travelers with consistency, warmth, and uncompromised quality.
